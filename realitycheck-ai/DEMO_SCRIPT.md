# REALITYCHECK AI - 2 to 3 Minute Hackathon Presentation Script

**Tagline:** *"Search the live web. Compare the evidence. Understand the claim."*

---

### [0:00 - 0:20] 1. The Problem
"Hello everyone! Every single day, millions of people use search engines to make buying decisions, verify breaking news, or research complex topics. 

But search engines only give you a wall of blue links. If you want to know if electric vehicles are actually cheaper to own, you have to open 15 tabs, read conflicting articles, filter out sponsored posts, and try to guess who to trust. 

Search engines give you raw results. They don't give you answers."

---

### [0:20 - 0:30] 2. Enter Question & Demonstration
"That is why we built **REALITYCHECK AI**. 

Let me show you a real-life example. I enter the question: 
*'Are electric vehicles cheaper to own than petrol cars?'* and click **Investigate Claim**."

---

### [0:30 - 0:50] 3. Live SerpApi Search & Query Expansion
"Behind the scenes, RealityCheck doesn't just run one search. It uses **SerpApi** to query Google Search live in real time. 

It automatically generates multi-perspective search queries—including counter-evidence queries like *'electric vehicle maintenance criticisms and battery replacement costs'*. This guarantees we don't fall into an echo chamber."

---

### [0:50 - 1:10] 4. Evidence Collection & Source Categorization
"As SerpApi streams back organic search results, top news stories, and answer boxes, RealityCheck cleans and classifies every source domain. 

It tags sources into trusted categories like *Official (.gov/.edu), News, Academic Research, Industry, Reviews,* and *Community Forums*."

---

### [1:10 - 1:30] 5. Claim Analysis & Verdict
"Next, RealityCheck breaks down the question into key factual claims. 

Notice our top summary dashboard: out of 4 claims investigated across 10 live web sources, 2 claims are **SUPPORTED**, 1 claim has **CONFLICTING EVIDENCE**, and 1 has **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**."

---

### [1:30 - 1:50] 6. Conflict Detection (The Core Differentiation)
"Here is where RealityCheck shines. Notice Claim #2: *'EV Maintenance Costs'*. 

Instead of hiding disagreement, RealityCheck shows **Supporting Evidence** from official government studies on the left, right alongside **Conflicting Evidence** highlighting expensive battery replacements on the right. 

We also provide an un-hyped **Confidence Rating** of 82% with an explicit breakdown of why."

---

### [1:50 - 2:10] 7. Evidence Graph & PDF Export
"To make complex investigations intuitive, RealityCheck features an interactive **Plotly Evidence Graph**. You can visually trace from the Question down to Claims, Evidence Snippets, and individual Web Sources. 

And with one click, you can download a publication-ready **PDF Investigation Report** containing executive summaries, claim tables, and clickable source citations."

---

### [2:10 - 2:30] 8. Why SerpApi Matters
"SerpApi is the core engine of RealityCheck. Without SerpApi's fast, structured, real-time Google Search SDK, an live AI fact-checking engine like this simply could not exist. SerpApi handles rate limits, structure parsing, and multi-query execution seamlessly."

---

### [2:30 - 2:45] 9. Closing Statement
"In summary:
Search engines give you results.
RealityCheck turns those results into an investigation.

Thank you!"
